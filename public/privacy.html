OUR PRIVACY PROMISE

Care comes first.

This prototype is for parents and legal guardians. Please do not upload photos of children without their guardian’s permission.

What we keep

We store the child’s first name, age, your dedication, optional sibling name, consent timestamp, book, and order details. Original photo bytes and order details are encrypted at rest. Generated artwork and PDFs contain personal information and remain private behind a book access token.

Photo deletion

Original uploads are deleted after seven days by an hourly cleanup while the server is running, and on startup. Use “Delete original photo” on your bookshelf to delete sooner. Deleting the original does not delete generated artwork, order records, or copies processed by an AI provider.

AI processing

When configured, the photo is sent to OpenAI to create a cartoon reference. That reference is used for illustrations. Provider retention policies apply separately. In demo mode, no photo is sent to an AI service. Parents should review every illustration and story before purchasing.

Orders and payments

Stripe hosts test checkout. Card details never pass through our server. Live charges and Lulu printing are disabled. Shipping details are stored only after a verified test payment event.

Before a public launch

This is a development prototype. A production operator must provide privacy contact details, deletion requests for all derived data and provider copies, backup retention rules, jurisdiction-specific consent requirements, and reviewed processor agreements before accepting real customer data.
